# Chapter 10 — Cost of Capital and WACC

**Exam weight: 3 questions / 7 points.**

## Core equations
- WACC = w_d × r_d × (1 − T) + w_p × r_p + w_s × r_s
- Preferred stock cost = annual preferred dividend / net issue proceeds
- Preferred cost with flotation = D_p / [P_p × (1 − F)]
- Retained-earnings cost using CAPM = r_f + beta × market risk premium
- Retained-earnings cost using Gordon growth = D1/P0 + g
- New common stock cost including flotation = D1/[P0 × (1 − F)] + g
- Debt cost may be represented by a bond's yield to maturity, with after-tax debt cost r_d(1 − T).

## Reminders
- Apply tax adjustment to **debt cost**, not preferred/common equity costs.
- Use financing weights that sum to 100%.
- 'Net proceeds' means proceeds after flotation costs.
